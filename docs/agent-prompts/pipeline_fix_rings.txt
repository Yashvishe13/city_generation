Fix three bugs in agent_scripts/nyc_midtown/pipeline.py. Nothing else - keep every other
behaviour exactly as it is.

tools/verify_area.sh nyc_midtown currently reports:

  ring_winding           1007 exterior rings are clockwise; extrusion comes out inside-out
  ring_shape             1777 rings are degenerate or repeat their first vertex
  ring_self_intersection 758 rings self-intersect

The three share one root cause: OSM closes a ring by repeating its first vertex, the
contract does not, and that repeated vertex also makes a naive self-intersection test fire
on nearly every building. Strip the duplicate first, then decide winding from the shoelace
sign of the coordinates you are about to emit, reversing when negative.

Also add `attrs.osm_id` to every node, carrying the source OSM id. Without it the
verifier's projection check cannot match your geometry back to the source, and that is the
check that catches a wrong origin or swapped axes.

Then run the pipeline, and run tools/verify_area.sh nyc_midtown. Both must be real runs:
your files are checked on disk for modification time and the verifier is re-run
independently, so a report of success the files do not support will be rejected.
